See this [Substance]({{< ref "/definitions/substance" >}}). ]Aristotle's doctrine of the four causes provides an account of the
different ways in which something can be explained.

## The Material Cause

The material cause is that out of which something is made.

## The Formal Cause

The formal cause is the form or structure that makes a thing what it is.

## The Efficient Cause

The efficient cause concerns the source or agent responsible for a thing's
coming into being.

## The Final Cause

The final cause concerns the end or purpose for which something exists.
