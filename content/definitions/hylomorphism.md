---
title: "Hylomorphism"
date: 2026-09-01
author: "The Eudemian"
draft: false
---

The metaphysical theory which holds that material [entities](/definitions/an-entity) are comprised of [matter](/definitions/matter) and [form](/definitions/form); of which comprise the one [substance](/definitions/substance).

Etymologically, '*hyle*' means 'matter': it is a word coined by Aristotle to refer to what which receives form. Likewise, '*morphus*' means 'form'. Hence: *hyle* + *morphus* translates to matter + form.
