---
title: "A Relational Distinction"
date: 2026-09-01
author: "The Eudemian"
draft: false
---

Any distinction which arises out of a [relation](/definitions/a-relation) between two things, which can be [virtual](/definitions/virtuality) (e.g., the relation between my thought and emotion) or [real](/definitions/reality) (e.g., relation of spin between the spin top and the table).
