---
title: "A Relation"
date: 2026-09-01
author: "The Eudemian"
draft: false
---

That which signifies towards another. 

For example, the speed and direction of the spinning top on a table is a relation between the spin top and its location relative to other objects in space (or to space itself): the spinning movement of the spin top is not a [part]() of it. On the contrary, the movements of the parts of an engine to facilitate it are parts of the engine as a whole and simultaneously are relations between the parts themselves. Thusly, a relation can be a part; but not all relations are parts.

Contrast with [a part]().
