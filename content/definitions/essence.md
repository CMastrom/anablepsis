---
title: "Essence"
date: 2026-09-01
author: "The Eudemian"
draft: false
---

The [quiddity]() or "whatness" which makes a being what is to be that kind of being as opposed to another.

The essence itself does not have existence: it is just the intelligibility of that kind of being. A [form]() provides it in existence as intelligible act.
