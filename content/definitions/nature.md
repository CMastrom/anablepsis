---
title: "Nature"
date: 2026-09-01
author: "The Eudemian"
draft: false
---

The [essence] of a being as understood through its principles of operation.

E.g., the essence of a human entails 'having an intellect'; and an intellect operating entails *thinking*. Thinking is an aspect of human nature; and having an intellect is an aspect of human essence.
