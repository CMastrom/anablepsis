---
title: "A Suppositum"
date: 2026-09-01
author: "The Eudemian"
draft: false
---

A completion of a [substance]() which posseses it.

E.g., this rock, this tree, this person, etc.

This completion is **not** the mere abstraction of the total of existence of an entity (e.g., this entire rock I will refer to as a suppositum); but, rather, it is an ontologically distinct completion from the substance which it completes. However, since a [part]() cannot be identical to its whole, the suppositum cannot be a part of the entity which it is the completion of.
