---
title: "Potency"
date: 2026-09-01
author: "The Eudemian"
draft: false
---

The real ways by which a being can be affected; or the real ways a being can innately extert. The former is *passive* potency (e.g., the flesh melting off the bone); and the latter is *active* potency (e.g., the exercising of the mind with thinking).

Contrast with [act]().
