---
title: "An Existent"
date: 2026-09-01
author: "The Eudemian"
draft: false
---

An entity which has existence extrinsically to its [nature](/definitions/nature); and consequently can never be existence itself taken as an entity (viz., an [essent](/definitions/an-essent)).

E.g., a chair depends on its legs to exist; iron depends on an act ordering its molecules; a tree depends on its soul being [educed](/definitions/eduction) out of matter; a material beings depends on matter which can receive its form; etc.
