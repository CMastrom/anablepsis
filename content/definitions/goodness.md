The equality of a being's [essence](/definitions/essence) and [existence](/definitions/existence) (viz., [esse](/definitions/esse)); or anything pertaining to such a proper relation.

Compare to [badness](/definitions/badness), [rightness](/definitions/rightness), [evil](/definitions/evil), and [wrongness](/definitions/wrongness).
