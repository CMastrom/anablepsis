---

The target substance or artifact of a thought. Contrast to [subject](/definitions/subject).

A subject is distinct from a [suppositum](/definitions/a-suppositum): the subject refers to the completion *as thought*, whereas the suppositum refers to the completion *in existence*. Likewise, the object is distinct from an [entity](/definitions/an-entity): the object is what substance *is thought*, whereas the entity is the substance that exists.
