---

The target [suppositum](/definitions/a-suppositum) of a thought. 

A subject is distinct from a [suppositum](/definitions/a-suppositum): the subject refers to suppositum *as thought*, whereas the suppositum refers to the completion *in existence*. Likewise, the object is distinct from an [entity](/definitions/an-entity): the object is what substance *is thought*, whereas the entity is the substance that exists.

See also: [object](/definitions/object).
