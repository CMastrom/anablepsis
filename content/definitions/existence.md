This term is used idiosyncratically here to refer to the bare property of existence. It cannot be captured with a strict definition; and is best understood through comparison.

The car has existence; it is a [being]() because it is a whole with existence; it is an [entity]() because it is a member of reality; and is an [existent]() because it lacks existence intrinsically to its [nature](). These terms are being used with odd precision to capture these different uses of 'being' which could be equivocated throughout the blog posts.
