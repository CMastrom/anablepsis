---
title: "A Formal Distinction"
date: 2026-09-01
author: "The Eudemian"
draft: false
---

Any distinction by [form](/definitions/form), which can be [virtual](/definitions/virtuality) (e.g., rationality vs. animality in humans) or [real](/definitions/reality) (e.g., the form of this chair is distinct from the form of this tree). Formal distinctions are closely connected conceptually to [substantial distinctions](/definitions/substantial-distinction), being that the latter bears the former.
