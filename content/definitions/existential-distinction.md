date: 2026-09-01

draft: false
---

A distinction in the [existential providence](/definitions/existential-providence) which makes it distinct from another existential, originative signature.

E.g., imagine there are two identical numbers on a screen but they are powered actively by two different electricity sources: the numbers are [ontologically dividuated](/definitions/ontological-dividuation) by their power sources. If the power sources were not actively sustaining the power supply for the digital number on the screen, then it would be metaphysically possible that the numbers could collapse ontologically into each other after being given power despite having two different sources.
