---
title: "Reality"
date: 2026-09-01
author: "The Eudemian"
draft: false
---

That which pertains to something in world as opposed to how a mind understands it; or the totality of what is the entire world. It is contrasted, in Thomism and similar metaphysical schools of thought, with *[virtual distinctions](/definitions/virtuality)*.

A real property or distinction is one which the object itself actually has independently of how a mind may understand it.
