---
title: "Existential Conferment"
date: 2026-09-01
author: "The Eudemian"
draft: false
---

The giving of [existence](/definitions/existence) to an [entity](/definitions/an-entity).

This is **not** to be confused with [change](/definitions/change): all change implies existential conferment, but not all existential conferment implies change. See [this post](existential-conferment-and-passive-potency) on existential conferment and passive potency.
